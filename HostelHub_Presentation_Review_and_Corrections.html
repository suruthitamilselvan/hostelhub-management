1. MongoDB / JPA Data Model (Corrected)

Database design for HostelHub implementation

users

  • _id (PK)
  • name
  • email
  • password
  • role [STUDENT|WARDEN|TECH]
  • faceEmbedding (128D)
  • isFaceVerified
  • studentId / roomNumber

complaints

  • _id (#CH1025)
  • studentId (FK)
  • title & description
  • category & priority
  • status / stage
  • locationBlock & room
  • slaBreached & isMerged
  • rating & photos

work_logs / spare_parts

  • _id (PK)
  • complaintId (FK)
  • technicianId (FK)
  • partName & quantity
  • unitCost & lineTotal
  • totalCost
  • beforePhoto & afterPhoto

3. State Pattern — Complaint Lifecycle (Corrected)

Controls ticket lifecycle transitions and handles Reopen & Duplicate Merge loops

1. SUBMITTED
Ticket created by student
2. UNDER REVIEW
Warden triages ticket
3. ASSIGNED
Tech assigned via Strategy
4. IN PROGRESS
Tech starts work & parts log
5. COMPLETED
After photo uploaded
6. CLOSED
Rated or merged duplicate

4. Observer Pattern — Complaint Notifications

Dispatches real-time updates to registered listeners

Subject: ComplaintSubject

- observers: List<ComplaintObserver>

+ registerObserver() | + notifyStatusChange()

Registered Observers

• StudentNotifier: Receives status transition alerts.

• TechnicianNotifier: Receives new task assignments.

• WardenNotifier (NEW): Receives SLA breach & duplicate alerts.
